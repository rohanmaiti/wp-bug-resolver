## Root cause
- Not found, because it didn't reproduce.
- The PDP loads now (no 404).
- The accordion header `.wiz-accordion-header` gets the browser default focus ring (`rgb(0,95,204) auto 1px`). With accessiBe "Highlight Focus" on, it gets `rgb(99,154,249) solid 2px`.
- No ancestor up to doc 17365 has a z-index or transform. The header (`[data-elementor-type=header]`) is `position: sticky; z-index: 150`.
- When the focused row was scrolled to sit right under the header (top 135px, header bottom 149px), the outline was hidden behind the header. Click → scroll gave the same result. No overlap seen.
