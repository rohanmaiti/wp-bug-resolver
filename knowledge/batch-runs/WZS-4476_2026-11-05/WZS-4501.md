## Bug card
- **Tenant / URL:** twos.wizcommerce.com: Contact Us (`/contact-us/`) and the claims product search (Raise Claim form, e.g. `/raise-claim/<id>/draft/`). Prod.
- **Expected vs actual:** Expected the page to stay at 100% when a field is focused. Actual: iOS Safari zooms in on focus and the layout looks cropped.
- **Repro:** Mobile Safari → Contact Us → tap Department / email / phone / Message. Claims: tap the product search.
- **Device / login:** iPhone Safari. Contact is public; claims need a logged-in buyer.
- **Attachments:** frame_0000s–0002s.jpg, ScreenRecording_11-04-2026 18-13-27_1.MP4. Duplicate root cause of WZS-4504.

## Root cause
iOS Safari auto-zooms when a focused field's font-size is under 16px. The Contact form fields were 14px. The claim product search inputs (`.wiz-claims-products__product-search-input` / `.wiz-claim-detail__product-search-input`) default to 15px in `widgets/claims/css/claims-form.css:2016` and `claims-detail.css:318`. WZS-4504 already added this to Site Settings (kit #6) › Custom CSS: `@media (max-width:767px){ input:not(...), select, textarea { font-size:16px !important } }`. That rule now wins on both pages: a matched-rules check on the claim input shows claims-form.css 15px overridden by the kit rule at 16px !important.
