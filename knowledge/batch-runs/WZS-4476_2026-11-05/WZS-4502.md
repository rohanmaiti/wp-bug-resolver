## Bug card
- **Tenant / URL:** twos.wizcommerce.com homepage, hamburger drawer (Elementor popup "sidebar" #1661, opened by #sidebar_button / icon cfd372d). Prod.
- **Expected vs actual:** Expected the header and footer to look normal after the drawer closes. Actual (as reported): the header and the bottom promo/footer strip keep a white bordered box and a dim overlay.
- **Repro:** Mobile Safari → homepage → open the hamburger → close it with X → look at the header and the bottom area.
- **Device / login:** iOS Safari (iPhone, Safari 26 floating bottom toolbar). Logged in (the drawer shows "Hello Wizcom Test New").
- **Attachments:** frame_0000s.jpg (before opening), frame_0001s.jpg (drawer open), frame_0003s.jpg (after closing), and a screen recording (MP4, not viewed).

## Root cause
- No site defect found.
- frame_0003s (after closing) matches frame_0000s (before opening) pixel for pixel in the page area. That includes the header (hamburger, logo, search, cart) and the hero.
- The "white bordered box" at the bottom is the hero's "SHOP VALENTINE'S DAY" button. Its design is a white outline button, and it shows in the before-open frame too.
- The "dim/faded overlay" is iOS Safari's own floating bottom toolbar and its fade. After a tap the toolbar buttons show solid white; before, they're translucent. That's browser UI, not page content.
- Live check in Chrome at 375px (same-origin iframe), opening and closing #1661:
  - After the X is clicked, the modal is `display:none`.
  - `document.activeElement` is `<body>`, so nothing in the header or footer keeps focus.
  - No fixed full-screen element stays visible.
  - The page looks normal.
- The only real highlight in the frames is the blue focus ring on the drawer's X (frame 0001s). WZS-4486 already fixed it in this batch, with the tagged :focus rule in #1661 Custom CSS.
